    How we play

    Double-six set, four players, seven tiles each. The player holding 6–6 opens with it each hand. Play clockwise, matching either open end. No drawing. You may pass only if none of your tiles fits.

    First to empty their hand wins. Four consecutive passes block the hand: the lowest remaining pip total wins; a tie scores no point. Each hand win earns one point. First to six wins the match. The host deals the next hand. These house rules apply to this first version.
